The basics

A custom-designed website of 5–10 pages, written and built for your business. The price includes professional copywriting, photos (yours or royalty-free stock photos), a basic text logo if you need one, a contact form that emails you, mobile-friendly design, local SEO setup, and launch on free hosting set up in your name. See everything that’s included.

There are no required monthly fees paid to us, ever. Your site is hosted on a free plan (usually Cloudflare Pages) in an account you own. The only ongoing cost most clients have is their domain name, which is typically $10–$20 per year and is paid directly to the domain registrar, not to us. Ongoing help is available if you want it, but it’s optional.

You do, completely. When your final payment is made, you own the code, the written content, the design, and the accounts it runs on. Your domain, hosting, and form service are registered in your name, and the source code lives in a repository you control. If you ever want to move on, you can take everything with you. Read more about owning your website.

We build fast, secure static websites using a repeatable process that IODEV has refined over years of software work. There are no expensive plugins, no databases to maintain, and no hosting bills to mark up. We keep the scope focused on what small businesses actually need, which lets us charge a flat, honest price instead of the hourly rates other agencies charge.

Your site is planned and built by IODEV, a U.S. software development company founded by Cory Keane. You’ll talk with Cory directly during your consultation and throughout the project.

It’s ideal for small businesses that need a professional website that explains what they do, builds trust, and gets people to call, book, or visit. That includes businesses with no website yet and businesses with an outdated one. It’s not the right fit for large online stores, member logins, or custom web applications. For those, we’ll point you to IODEV’s custom development services.

Features and technology

A static website is made of ready-to-serve pages instead of being built on the fly by a database. That makes it very fast, very secure, and free to host. There are no plugins to update and nothing for hackers to break into. It’s the same approach many large companies use for their marketing sites.

SEO and getting found

We do the one-time groundwork that helps local customers find you. That includes keyword research for your services and service area, optimized page titles, headings, and descriptions, structured data, an XML sitemap, Google Search Console setup, and help setting up or improving your Google Business Profile. Ongoing SEO work is available separately but isn’t required.

Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings. What we can promise is a fast, well-structured site with strong SEO foundations, which is what Google recommends. Rankings also depend on your competition, your reviews, and your Google Business Profile, and we’ll give you a plan for all three.

We build sites to be easy for both search engines and AI assistants to understand. That means clear content, accurate business details, and structured data that describes who you are, what you do, and where you work. There’s no special trick beyond that, and anyone who promises one is overselling.
