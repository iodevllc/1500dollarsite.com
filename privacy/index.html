This privacy policy explains how 1500DollarSite.com, a service of IODEV (“we,” “us”), collects and uses information when you visit 1500dollarsite.com or contact us.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit our consultation form or newsletter signup, we collect what you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, business name, and message.

Usage information. We use privacy-friendly analytics to understand how many people visit our site and which pages they view. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a consultation
  • To provide the services you hire us for
  • To send occasional emails if you subscribe to our list (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To improve our website and services

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate this website, including a form-processing service (such as Formspree) to deliver form submissions to us, an email service for our newsletter, and our website host. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to us.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. Our analytics and hosting providers may use minimal technical cookies or similar technologies to operate.

Data retention

We keep inquiry information as long as needed to respond to you and for reasonable business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any message. To access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, send us a message through our contact form.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for business owners and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

Contact
