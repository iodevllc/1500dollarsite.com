Accessibility Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

  1. Home
  2. Accessibility Policy

1500DollarSite.com, a service of IODEV (“we,” “us”), wants everyone to be able to use 1500dollarsite.com, including people who use assistive technology such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, voice control, or a keyboard alone.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

What we do

  • Use clear headings, landmarks, and page structure so content makes sense to screen readers
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Make every link, button, menu, and form usable with a keyboard, with a “skip to content” link at the top of each page
  • Label every form field and announce form results to assistive technology
  • Choose text sizes and color combinations that are easy to read
  • Respect your device’s “reduce motion” setting by turning off decorative animations
  • Write in plain language and keep pages fast on phones and slow connections

We review the site for accessibility when we add or change pages, using automated tools along with manual keyboard and screen reader checks.

Known limitations

Some parts of the experience rely on third-party services, such as the form service that delivers our contact and consultation forms. We choose providers with accessibility in mind, but we don’t control their products. If any part of the site gets in your way, please tell us and we’ll find another way to help.

Feedback and assistance

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or have a suggestion for making it better, send us a message through our contact form. Please include the page address and a short description of the problem. We’ll reply within one business day and, if you need it, provide the information you were looking for in another format.

Accessibility in the websites we build

Accessibility basics are part of every website we build: readable fonts, good color contrast, alt text, and keyboard-friendly navigation. See what’s included in every project.
