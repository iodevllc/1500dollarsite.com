Website planning checklist

Get organized before you build

Our free checklist walks you through everything to think about before you build or rebuild a website. It will save you time, money, and back-and-forth no matter who builds your site. It covers seven areas:

  • Goals: the one action you want visitors to take, and who you want to reach
  • Business basics: your name, contact details, hours, and credentials
  • Pages: which pages you need, and which services deserve their own
  • Content and photos: service descriptions, your story, reviews, and images
  • Domain and accounts: making sure your domain and Google profile are in your name
  • Getting found: the searches, cities, and directories that matter to you
  • Questions to ask any web designer: price, ongoing costs, ownership, and more
