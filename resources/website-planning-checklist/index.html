Small Business Website Planning Checklist

Everything to think through before you build a website, whether you work with us, a builder, or anyone else.

  1. Home
  2. Resources
  3. Website Planning Checklist

Use this checklist to get organized before you build or rebuild your website. It will save you time, money, and back-and-forth no matter who builds your site.

1. Goals

  • What is the one main action you want visitors to take? (Call, book, request a quote, visit, buy)
  • Who is your ideal customer, and what are they searching for?
  • What questions do customers ask you most often?
  • What makes you different from your top three competitors?

2. Business basics

  • Official business name, exactly as you want it to appear
  • Phone number and email address for inquiries
  • Physical address, or the list of cities you serve
  • Business hours
  • Licenses, certifications, insurance, and memberships worth mentioning

3. Pages

  • Home
  • About
  • Services (and which services deserve their own page)
  • Contact
  • Reviews or testimonials
  • Optional: Gallery, FAQ, Service Area, Pricing, Blog, Careers

4. Content and photos

  • A short description of each service
  • Your story: how and why you started
  • Three to five customer reviews you have permission to use
  • Photos of your work, your team, and your location (phone photos are fine if they’re clear and well lit)
  • Your logo, in the highest-quality file you have

5. Domain and accounts

  • Do you have a domain? Is it registered in your name?
  • Do you have a Google Business Profile? Do you have the login?
  • Do you have a professional email address (like you@yourbusiness.com)?

6. Getting found

  • List the top five to ten phrases customers might search to find you
  • List the cities, suburbs, or neighborhoods you want to be found in
  • Note any directories that matter in your industry (like Houzz, Avvo, or Yelp)

7. Questions to ask any web designer

  • What exactly is included in the price?
  • Who writes the content?
  • What will I pay every month or year after launch?
  • Who owns the website, domain, and hosting accounts?
  • How many revisions are included?
  • What happens if I want to leave?
  • How long will it take?
