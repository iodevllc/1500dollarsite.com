Website builders look cheap and easy at first. Here's what they really cost over time, and when a professionally built site you own makes more sense.

The short answer

Website builders are a fine choice if you enjoy designing and writing your own site and don't mind a monthly bill. If you'd rather have a professional do it, own the result, and stop paying every month, a $1,500 custom site usually costs less within about five years.

When a website builder makes sense

Builders like Wix and Squarespace are genuinely good products. If you enjoy design, like tinkering, have time to write all of your own content, and are comfortable paying a subscription indefinitely, a builder can work well.

When it doesn’t

Most small business owners we talk to don’t want to become web designers. They’ve started a builder site, gotten stuck halfway, or ended up with something that looks a little homemade. Meanwhile, the monthly charge keeps renewing.

With a $1,500 site from us:

  • A professional does the work. We plan, write, design, and build the site.
  • You own it. The code is in a repository you control, and hosting is in your name.
  • The cost stops. After launch, there’s no required monthly fee.
