Yes, we’re an agency too

1500DollarSite.com is a service of IODEV, a software development agency founded by Cory Keane. We’ve spent years building websites and custom software for businesses of all sizes. We started 1500DollarSite.com because we kept seeing small businesses get a bad deal from agencies: huge quotes for simple sites, slow timelines, and retainers that never end.

Why other agencies cost so much

Many agencies have account managers, strategists, designers, developers, and project managers, plus offices and sales teams. A small business site ends up carrying all of that overhead. Many also build on platforms that need ongoing hosting and maintenance, which turns a one-time project into a monthly bill.

What we do differently

We kept the parts of an agency project that matter most for small businesses and dropped the rest:

  • One published price instead of a custom proposal and rounds of quoting
  • A focused discovery interview instead of weeks of workshops
  • You work with the founder, not an account manager who passes your notes along
  • Copywriting, design, and SEO done by people who’ve done it many times
  • Static sites that are fast, secure, and free to host, so there’s nothing to resell you monthly
  • Full ownership at launch, with everything in your name

When you need more

Some projects really do need more than a 5–10 page website. For larger sites, IODEV offers custom pricing. For custom web applications, e-commerce, and integrations, IODEV offers custom development, so you don’t have to start over with someone else.
