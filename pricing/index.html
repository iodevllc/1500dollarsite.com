Your only ongoing cost

Your domain name (like yourbusiness.com) is renewed each year directly with a registrar such as Cloudflare or Namecheap. Expect about $10–$20 per year. We'll help you register it in your name if you don't have one.

Why hosting is free

We build static websites, which can be hosted on free plans from providers like Cloudflare Pages that allow commercial use. If your site ever outgrows a free plan, which is rare for small businesses, you'd pay the host directly.
