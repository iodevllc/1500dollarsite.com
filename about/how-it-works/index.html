Our process

From first call to launch in about a month

A clear, step-by-step process designed to take as little of your time as possible. You focus on your business, and we handle the website.

  1. Home
  2. About
  3. How It Works

  1. Free consultation

    20–30 minutes · Free

    Fill out the consultation form, and Cory will email you within one business day with a link to schedule a call. We'll talk about your business, your goals, and what your site needs. If it's a fit, you'll get a fixed price, usually $1,500. If it isn't, we'll tell you honestly and point you in a better direction.

  2. Kickoff and $750 deposit

    Day 1

    When you're ready, we send a simple agreement and an invoice for the $750 deposit. Once it's paid, your project is on the calendar.

  3. Discovery and content

    Week 1

    You fill out a short questionnaire and join a 30–45 minute discovery interview. We learn about your services, customers, service area, and what makes you different. Send us any photos, logos, or reviews you have. Then we plan your pages and write all the content.

  4. Design and build

    Weeks 2–3

    We design and build your site and send you a private preview link. You can click through it on your own phone and computer, just like the real thing.

  5. Review and revisions

    Weeks 3–4

    Tell us what you'd like changed, from wording to photos to layout. Two full rounds of revisions are included, and most clients only need one.

  6. Approval and final $750

    Launch week

    Once you're happy, you approve the site and pay the final $750.

  7. Launch and handover

    Launch day

    We launch your site on free hosting, connect your domain, set up your forms, submit your sitemap to Google and Bing, and help with your Google Business Profile. Then we hand over everything, including code, accounts, and a simple guide, in your name.

  8. 30 days of launch support

    After launch

    For 30 days after launch, we fix any issues and answer questions at no charge. After that, you can request updates as needed, or never hear from us again. It's your call.

What we need from you

  • A 20–30 minute consultation call
  • A short questionnaire about your business
  • A 30–45 minute discovery interview
  • Any photos, logo, or reviews you have (optional)
  • Feedback on your preview site
  • Access to your domain, or help choosing one

What you'll have at the end

  • A live, custom website
  • Hosting, forms, and domain in your name
  • Your site's full source code
  • Local SEO foundations in place
  • A simple guide to everything
