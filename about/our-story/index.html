Why we started 1500DollarSite.com

At IODEV, we've spent years building custom software and websites for businesses of every size. Over and over, we met small business owners stuck in the same place: they knew they needed a website, but their options were all frustrating.

They could spend weekends wrestling with a website builder. They could sign up for a "free" website with a monthly bill that never ends. Or they could pay a typical agency thousands of dollars. Many simply gave up and relied on a Facebook page.

We knew there was a better way. Using modern static website technology and a process refined over many projects, we can build a professional, custom website for a flat $1,500, and hand the owner complete control of it.

What we believe

You should own what you pay for

Your website is a business asset. When you pay for it, it should be yours, with no strings and no ransom.

Prices should be simple

One clear price, published right on our website. No surprise invoices and no long proposals.

Honesty beats a sale

If we're not the right fit, we'll say so and point you somewhere better.

Small businesses deserve great work

The neighborhood bakery deserves a website as well built as a big company's.
