How it works

From first call to launch in about a month

A clear process designed to take as little of your time as possible, usually two to four hours in total. You focus on your business, and we handle the website.

See the full process

  1. Free consultation

    20–30 minutes · Free

    We talk about your business and what your site needs. If it's a fit, you get a fixed price, usually $1,500.

  2. Kickoff and $750 deposit

    Day 1

    A simple agreement and a $750 deposit put your project on the calendar.

  3. Content, design, and revisions

    Weeks 1–4

    We interview you, write your content, and build your site. You review a private preview, with two rounds of revisions included.

    Launch week

    You approve and pay the final $750. We launch, hand over everything in your name, and support you for 30 days.
